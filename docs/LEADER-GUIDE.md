# Sectional – quick guide for leaders

## 1. Install it (once)

1. Open the link you were sent in **Safari** on your iPad.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. From now on, open **Sectional** from your home screen. It works without Wi-Fi.

The four buttons along the bottom are **🎤 Rehearsal · 🎼 Songs · 🗂 Past · ❔ Help**.

## 2. Add a song

1. Tap **🎼 Songs**, type the song title and tap **+ Add**.
2. Tap **+ Add score (PDF)** and choose the score (from Files, email attachments saved to Files, or iCloud Drive). Add both the choir + piano and the choir-only versions if you have them.
3. Tap **+ Add tracks** and choose the MP3s. You can pick several at once. The app guesses names like "Alto" from the file name. Tap ✎ to rename one.

## 3. Make timestamps (once per track)

1. Next to a track, tap **⏱ Timestamps**.
2. Tap **▶ Play** and follow the score.
3. Each time a rehearsal letter or main phrase starts, tap the big orange **MARK** button.
4. When the song ends, type the **bar number** and **letter** for each mark (e.g. 15 and A). Words are optional.
5. Tap a time to check it. If it's a little early or late, use **−1s / +1s**.

Tips:
- Did the part tracks come from the same recording? Open the Alto track, tap **⧉ Copy from another track**, choose Full mix, then check a few times and nudge them.
- Already have the table from the "2027 Time Stamps" doc? Tap **📋 Paste a table**.

## 4. Run a rehearsal

1. Tap **🎤 Rehearsal** → **Start a new rehearsal**, tap your section (e.g. Alto) and tick the songs.
2. In **▶ Play**:
   - **Track:** tap the track you want (the buttons use the track names, e.g. Full mix, Alto). It stays chosen until you tap another.
   - **Start from:** tap **▶ From the top**, a letter (**A**, **B**, **C**…), or type a bar number and tap **▶ Bar**.
     Letters and bars need that track's timestamps. Without them, only "From the top" works.
   - Use the big **⏹ Stop**, **↺ Again** and **⏪ Back 5s** buttons.
3. **Voice (optional):** tap **🎤 Speak** and say e.g. "letter B", "bar 32" or "alto bar 32". Saying a track name switches to that track.
   You can also type the same words in the box and tap **Go**.
   Using a Bluetooth speaker? The iPad may move the sound off the speaker when the microphone is used. If so, turn the voice button off in **❔ Help** and use the buttons.
4. Tap **📖 Score** to show the score full screen. **🎵 Player** shows or hides the track and letter buttons.
   - **✏️ Mark up** lets you draw on the score: **✏️ Pen**, **🖍 Highlight**, **🔤 Text** (tap where the note goes), **🧽 Eraser**, **↶ Undo**. Tap **✋ Scroll** to move the page again. Your marks are saved on this iPad.
5. **📝 Notes**: type anything worth remembering.
6. **✓ Covered**: tap the letters you worked on and add any bars.
7. **🔧 Fix next time**: add things to work on. Tick them when they're sorted. Anything left unticked shows up again next time.

## 5. Finish and share

Tap **Finish & share**. Check the summary (you can edit it), then tap **📤 Share** to send it by email, Messages or WhatsApp. Past rehearsals stay under **🗂 Past**.

## 6. No sound from the speaker?

1. Swipe down from the top-right corner to open **Control Center**.
2. Press and hold the music box, tap the **AirPlay** icon (a triangle with circles), and choose your speaker.
3. Turn the volume up on both the iPad and the speaker.
4. Still nothing? Close Sectional fully (swipe it away) and open it again.
5. Disconnect any earbuds. The iPad sends sound to only one place at a time.

## 7. Keep a backup

Your songs and notes are kept only on your iPad. Every few weeks, go to **❔ Help** → **💾 Back up everything** and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. Use **Restore a backup** to load it on a new iPad.
