# Sectional Practice: quick guide (phone)

A simple practice app for your phone: play your part's tracks, start from a letter or bar,
slow them down and hear your starting note. It starts empty: you add your own songs.

Address: https://novadiva.github.io/sectionalapp/phone/

## 1. Install it (once)

**iPhone:** open the link in **Safari** → tap **Share** (square with an arrow; you may need to tap **⋯** first)
→ **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
**Android phone:** open the link in **Chrome** → **⋮** menu → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

Always open **Practice** from its home screen icon. It works without Wi-Fi.

## 2. Choose your part

The first time, the app asks **"Which part do you sing?"**. Tap your part (e.g. **Alto 1**), then **Save**.
You then see only your tracks, plus tracks for everyone (full mix, piano). Change it any time in **❔ Help**.

## 3. Add a song

1. On **🎵 Songs**, type the song title and tap **+ Add**.
2. Tap **+ Add tracks** and choose the MP3s (full mix and your part) from Files, iCloud Drive or a saved
   email attachment. The app guesses each track's part from its name; check the box under each track.
3. Optional: have the score with the track times printed on it (the "with timestamps" PDF)?
   Tap **+ Add score**. The app reads the times so you can start from a letter or bar. The score isn't shown on the phone.
4. Optional: under **🎹 Starting notes**, tap **+ Set**, tap keys to find your note, then **Save**.
5. Tap **✓ Done: practise this song**.

## 4. Practise

- **Track:** tap the track you want (e.g. **Full mix** or **Alto 1**). Switching while it plays carries on from the same place.
- **Start from:** **▶ From the top**, a letter (**A**, **B**…), or type a bar number and tap **▶ Bar**.
- **⏪ 5s**, **▶ Play / ⏸ Pause**, **↺ Again** (back to where you last started), **⏹ Stop**.
- **Speed:** tap **90%** … **50%** to slow down (the pitch stays the same). **Normal** goes back.
- **Starting note:** tap the orange **▶ Alto 1: A4** button, or **🎹 Keyboard** for any note (**◀ Lower** / **Higher ▶** change octave).
- To change tracks, the score or starting notes later: **⚙ Set up this song** at the bottom.

## 5. Backup

Songs are kept only on your phone. In **❔ Help**, tap **💾 Back up everything** and save the file to Files or
iCloud Drive. **⤒ Restore a backup** puts it on a new phone. It can also restore an **"everything"** backup
from the iPad Sectional app (songs, tracks, times and starting notes).
